    Characters: races, classes, stats and levelling¶

    How a player character is built and how it grows from level 1 to 91. Paths are relative to xania/src/. The Characters assessment applies these rules to every race and class.

    Creation¶

    1. Race and class. There are 10 PC races and 4 classes. The race sets starting stats (MudImpl.cpp:831), and the creation-point total starts at the race's cost.
    2. Skills. Every character gets rom basics plus its <class> basics group free. The player then takes the <class> default package (40 points) or customises, buying groups and skills at their per-class cost (skills.cpp, group_add). Points are capped at 200.
    3. XP per level is fixed at creation from the total points (see below). Spending more on skills makes every level dearer.
    4. Entering the game (MudImpl.cpp:984):
    5. +3 to the class prime stat, except for dragons;
    6. 20 HP, 100 mana, 100 move;
    7. 3 trains and 5 practices;
    8. if points are below 40, extra trains of (40 − points + 1) / 2.

    Classes¶

    From Class.cpp:

    Class Prime stat HP roll per level Mana factor To-hit AC at level 0 → 32 Free weapon skill
    mage int 6–8 10 −1 → −47 dagger
    cleric wis 7–10 8 −1 → −51 mace
    knight str 10–14 6 −1 → −61 sword
    barbarian con 13–17 3 −1 → −63 axe
    • To-hit AC is interpolated by level and drives the chance to hit (see the combat guide).
    • Skill adept is 75% for every class. That's the most practising can teach; using a skill can improve it further.

    Races¶

    From pc_race_table in Races.cpp:147. Each race has: - a creation-point cost; - an XP multiplier for each class; - up to 5 bonus skills; - base and maximum stats; - a body size.

    The table is listed on the Characters page. Bonus skills are cheaper to learn: difficulty −1 and available by level 30 at the latest (skills.cpp:566).

    Race also merges NPC-style flags: elves resist charm and are vulnerable to iron, dwarves resist poison and disease, and so on (Races.cpp, race_table).

    XP per level¶

    exp_per_level(points) (skills.cpp:259) works like this: - Below 40 points: 1000 × class multiplier. - From 40 points: start at 1000. The next 10 points add 500, the 10 after that add 500 more, and the step then doubles for every following pair of 10s. Partial tens add pro rata. - The result is multiplied by the race's class multiplier and capped at 65,500.

    A character levels when exp ≥ exp_per_level × (level + 1) (update.cpp:586). Every level costs the same, and reaching 91 takes 90 × that.

    Points XP per level (×1.00)
    40 (human, default) 1,000
    45 (elf, dwarf) 1,250
    50 (eagle, orc) 1,500
    55 (minotaur, half-elf) 1,750
    60 (hobbit, wolf) 2,000
    80 (dragon) 4,000

    Stats¶

    Five stats, each from 3 to 25. Their effects come from the bonus tables in Stats.cpp:

    Stat Effect
    str str_app: hitroll and damroll bonus (Char.cpp:439), carry weight (handler.cpp:156), heaviest weapon you can wield (handler.cpp:175)
    int int_app.learn: practice gain (act_info.cpp:1604) and learn-by-use chance; mana gain above 15
    wis wis_app: practices per level; mana gain above 15
    dex dex_app.defensive: added to AC while awake (Char.cpp:200); movement gain
    con con_app.hitp: HP bonus per level; movement gain; HP regeneration (update.cpp:336)

    Limits: - Trainable maximum: the race maximum, +2 for the prime stat, capped at 25 (handler.cpp:117). - Effective maximum with spells and items: the race maximum +4, or +6 for the prime stat (Char.cpp:192).

    Levelling up¶

    advance_level (update.cpp:480) runs once per level gained:

    • HP: (con_app.hitp + random(class min..max)) × 9 / 10, at least 1.
    • Mana: random(0 .. factor² × (max(0, wis−15) + 2·max(0, int−15))), then (that + 150) / 300 + factor / 2. So below 16 int and wis a mage gains a flat 5 mana and a barbarian 1.
    • Move: random(1 .. (con + dex) / 6) × 9 / 10, at least 6.
    • Practices: wis_app.practice.
    • Trains: +1. A train buys +1 to a stat (up to its trainable maximum), +10 HP or +10 mana (act_move.cpp:1020).

    Practising skills¶

    Practising at a guild (act_info.cpp:1561) costs a practice. It raises the skill by int_app.learn / difficulty, where difficulty is the class's rating for that skill, up to the 75% adept limit. Beyond that, skills improve only through use (Learning.cpp).
