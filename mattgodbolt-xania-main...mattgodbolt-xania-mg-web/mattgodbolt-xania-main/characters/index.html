    Characters by level¶

    How each class and race grows from level 1 to 91: the XP each level costs, the HP, mana and movement it brings, and how quickly stats max out.

    4×XP per level, dearest vs cheapest build

    A dragon needs 4× the XP per level of a human¶

    A dragon needs 4,000 XP per level and a human 1,000, so reaching level 91 takes 360,000 against 90,000 XP. Two things drive it. Creation points: every 10 points above the default 40 raise the cost steeply (exp_per_level), and dragons cost 40 points and hobbits cost 20 points. Class multipliers: minotaur knight ×1.5, elf barbarian ×1.5 and eagle barbarian ×1.5.

    Barbarians finish with 1.5× a mage's HP¶

    At level 91 a human barbarian has about 2,272 HP, a knight 1,923, a cleric 1,632 and a mage 1,534. 700–740 HP of every build comes from trains spent on HP once stats are maxed, about the same for every class, so the gap comes from the per-level roll (barbarian 13–17, mage 6–8) and, for barbarians, the extra con of their prime stat.

    An elf mage ends with 31% more mana than a wolf mage¶

    Expensive races buy very little extra HP¶

    Within a class, the dearest races barely out-grow the cheapest. A dragon pays 4× a human's XP per level for 1%–4% more HP at level 91. Its other benefits (bonus skills such as breath weapons) aren't counted here, but the price is steep. Each dot is one build; further left is cheaper, higher is tougher.

    Stats are maxed by level 21, then only the caps matter¶

    With 3 trains at creation and one per level, every build can max all five stats by level 17–21 (latest: dragon mage). After that, stats stop differentiating builds except through their caps: an elf's con tops out at 15 (17 for barbarians), which is why elves have the lowest HP in every class.

    Method¶

    How these numbers are calculated
    • Builds: every PC race × class with the class's default skill package (40 creation points) plus the race's points. Customising costs more points and so more XP per level.
    • XP per level = exp_per_level(points) (skills.cpp:259) × the race's class multiplier. It's the same at every level, so reaching level 91 takes 90× that.
    • Stats start at the race's base +3 to the class prime stat (not dragons, MudImpl.cpp). Each stat can be trained to the race maximum, +2 for the prime stat (handler.cpp get_max_train), capped at 25.
    • Trains: 3 at creation and 1 per level. Assumed spent on stats until all are maxed (prime first, then con, str, dex, wis, int), then on +10 HP each.
    • Per-level gains are the expected values of advance_level (update.cpp:480): HP = (con bonus + class roll) × 0.9; mana from the class factor, int and wis above 15; move from con + dex; practices from wis. Starting values are 20 HP, 100 mana, 100 move.
    • Equipment, spells and skills that raise stats aren't included.
