    Equipment by level¶

    Armour and other worn gear: how much protection and hitroll/damroll the world offers at each level from 1 to 91, where the gaps are, and which items break the item-points rule. Weapons have their own page.

    A fully kitted character reaches 407 AC by level 91¶

    Wearing the best obtainable piece in every slot gives about 79 AC at level 10, 192 at 30, 269 at 60 and 407 at 91. Every slot has an armour piece by level 8 (ears is last). For comparison, the median combat mob at levels 86–90 has an AC of only 52 (see Mobs): players can armour far beyond what mobs carry.

    Gear adds up to +74 damroll by level 91¶

    Gear chosen for offence gives +32 hitroll and +26 damroll at level 30, and +67/+74 at 91. Damroll is added to every hit (fight.cpp:528), so by the late game gear damroll is comparable to the best weapon's dice average (52.5 at level 91, see Weapons). The longest stretch with no improvement is levels 35–45.

    160 items break the item-points rule¶

    The rule allows level/10 + 1 points; 160 obtainable items exceed it, most from UnderDark (19), Camelot (14) and The Seminary (13). The furthest over is a cloak (Imladris, level 23): 19 points against 3. Movement and HP bonuses count one point per 7, so large Move +100 items blow the budget quickly. Above level 91, 1 item also breaks it, including One of Angela's friendship bracelets (Mos Eisley) at 1058 points.

    Armour-class bonuses are never counted against the rule¶

    AFFECT_DATA::worth (AFFECT_DATA.cpp:86) is meant to charge a point for each −1 of AC or saving-throw bonus, but its test reads if (modifier < 0) if (modifier > 0), which can never be true. So Ac and Saving* affects are free. Counting them, 108 more items would be over the limit.

    Method¶

    How these numbers are calculated
    • Gear: obtainable (placed by a reset), wearable, non-weapon items of level ≤ 91: 1040 items (47 more have no source).
    • AC of an item = the mean of its four armour values (armour objects only, handler.cpp apply_ac) plus any Ac affect (negative is better). Higher here means more protection. A PC's own AC starts at 0 (Char.hpp) and only dex and gear lower it.
    • Kits: for each level, the best obtainable item per slot that a character of that level may wear, judged by AC (or by hitroll + damroll). Two-slot locations (neck, wrist, finger) take two of the best. Items with several wear flags are considered for each slot. Real characters won't have every best item; this is the ceiling the world offers.
    • Points rule (entity_balance.cpp report_object): stats +1 each, hitroll/damroll +1 each (half for weapons), HP/mana/move +1 per 7, weapon flags +1 each; allowed level/10 + 1. AC and saving-throw affects should count too but never do (AFFECT_DATA.cpp:86).
    • There are no class restrictions on wearing items, only level and alignment flags.
