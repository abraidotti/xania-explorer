    Combat & experience by level¶

    What a straight melee fight against level-appropriate mobs looks like for each class at every level from 1 to 91: how often each side hits, how long fights last, how many kills a level takes, and when fights turn deadly.

    A model, not a simulation

    Expected values from the C++ formulas, for a human of each class with the best gear the world offers at that level. Spells, buffs such as haste and sanctuary, resistances and mob skills like bash aren't modelled, so casters look weaker here than they play. See the Method at the end.

    Every level takes about the same number of kills¶

    A human needs 1,000 XP per level at every level, and a level-appropriate kill is worth about 133 XP at level 10 and 104 at level 80, so a level takes roughly 7 kills in levels 6–30 and 9 from 70 up. XP depends only on the level gap, not class, so this is the same for every class. The rise late on is xp_compute deliberately cutting XP above level 35. Races with dearer XP (up to 4× for dragons, see Characters) multiply these counts.

    Full XP requires 1.5 hours per level, more than any class spends fighting¶

    xp_compute scales every kill by how long you've played per level: below an average of 1.5 hours per level the reward shrinks, down to a third (below level 15 there's a softer floor, and levels 1–2 always get full credit). Reaching level 91 at full XP therefore takes about 135 hours of play, whatever the class. Pure combat time is much less: knight 24 h, barbarian 28 h, cleric 42 h, mage 54 h. For every class, combat never fills the clock, so the time rule, not the fighting, sets the pace.

    Fights stretch to 75+ rounds by level 91¶

    At level 10 a knight needs about 12 rounds (35 s) and a mage 32. By level 60 that's 54 and 124 rounds, and at 91 75 and 136. Mob HP grows faster than player damage: the median combat mob at levels 86–90 has about 7,450 HP. Haste (an extra attack per round) and spells would shorten these a lot.

    Melee turns deadly for mages from level 48¶

    A mob counts as deadly when its expected damage over the whole fight exceeds your maximum HP. More than half the mobs at your level are deadly in melee, for good, for mage from level 48, cleric from level 48, knight from level 73, barbarian from level 73. Casters really fight with spells and buffs (sanctuary alone cuts damage taken to 62%), so this marks where melee stops being an option, not where the class fails.

    Players' accuracy dips in the middle levels as mob dex climbs¶

    Players land most blows early, but accuracy dips through the middle levels: a mage's chance bottoms out around level 58 at 43%. Mob stats are 11 + level/4, so their dex reaches the 20s by level 40 and the dex bonus to their AC (dex_app) grows faster than a player's to-hit. Mobs, meanwhile, rarely hit a fully armoured player: about 6%–20% per swing against a knight, because player AC from gear dwarfs anything mobs wear.

    Method¶

    How these numbers are calculated

    For each class (human, default build, see Characters) at each level 1–91, fight every combat mob within ±2 levels (the mobs a player of that level would hunt), using the C++ formulas:

    • the player wields the best weapon of a starting type usable at that level (see Weapons) and wears the best-AC kit (see Equipment), including whatever hitroll/damroll that kit carries;
    • weapon and combat skills are practised to adept (75%) when learned and improve with use to 100% by level 30 (an assumption);
    • mobs use their real stats: level-based stats and skills (db.cpp create_mobile, Char::get_skill), file hitroll/damroll/AC, the weapon or shield their resets equip.

    Expected values are used throughout (hit chances, extra attacks, parry/dodge/block, damage). Spells, affects (sanctuary, haste…), resistances, special procedures and mob skills like bash are not modelled, so mages and clerics look weaker here than they play: see Skills & spells for spell damage.

    • Hit chance (HitChance.cpp): attacker to-hit AC = interpolate(level, class AC at 0, at 32) × weapon skill % − hitroll; ratio to the defender's AC (made positive, minimum 1), clamped 10–95%. Then the defender's parry (if armed), dodge and shield block (if a shield is worn) each get skill/3 + level difference (minimum 5%).
    • Attacks per round: 1 + second attack (skill/2 %) + third attack (skill/4 %); mobs also get +1 if Fast, and their third only if the second landed.
    • Damage per hit: weapon dice × skill % (× enhanced damage) + damroll; mobs fighting unarmed use their damage dice, with the dice bonus as damroll.
    • Mobs fought at level L: every combat mob within ±2 levels, weighted by population.
    • XP per kill: xp_compute for a neutral, solo player with full played-time credit (at least 1.5 h played per level; less and every kill is worth proportionally less, down to a third). Kills per level = 1,000 XP (human) ÷ mean XP per kill.
    • Deadly mobs: share of that population whose expected damage over the whole fight exceeds the player's maximum HP.
    • Combat minutes per level = kills × rounds × 3 s; resting, travel and searching are not included.
