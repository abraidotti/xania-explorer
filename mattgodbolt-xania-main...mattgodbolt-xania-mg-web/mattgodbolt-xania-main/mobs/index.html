    6 levels have no combat mob at all¶

    No combat mob exists at levels 63, 67, 77, 84, 87, 89. Across single levels the population ranges from 0 to 237 (CV 1.14).

    9 areas have most mobs outside their advertised level range¶

    Of the 1354 combat mobs in areas that advertise a level range, 19% fall outside it. In 9 areas, most of the mobs do: Machine Dreams (1–5, actually 8), Nirvana (30–35, actually 8–40), Graveyard (5–10, actually 3–6) and Troll Den (10–15, actually 3–13). Players choosing areas by the areas list will be misled.

    Method¶

    How these numbers are calculated
    • 1643 mob prototypes: 49 above hero, 1445 combat, 23 never spawns, 126 service. Only combat mobs (spawned, not shopkeepers/trainers/practicers/healers/gain, level ≤ 91) are analysed.
    • Population = the steady-state number of instances the resets maintain: min(world cap, sum of room caps) over the mob's M resets (db.cpp reset_room). It ignores wandering and kills, so it's what a freshly reset world holds.
    • Evenness: CV (stdev ÷ mean) and Gini, both 0 when perfectly even.
    • HP and damage are dice averages. Damage dice only apply when the mob fights unarmed (fight.cpp:465); armed mobs use their weapon. AC is the mean of the four armour classes (more negative = better).
    • Outliers: HP or damage above 2.5× or below 0.4× the median of combat mobs within ±2 levels.
    • Flags are as written in the area file (race flags are merged at load and aren't counted).
