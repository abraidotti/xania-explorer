    Levelling path¶

    Where a character of each level can go for experience: how many areas suit them, how much XP the world offers per reset, which areas carry each stretch of levels, and how far away they are.

    133 → 2.6levels' worth of XP per world reset, level 3 → 88

    The XP on offer falls 50× between level 3 and level 88¶

    Killing every level-appropriate mob once, in a freshly reset world, is worth about 133 levels of XP to a level-3 character, 52 at level 20, 14 at 40, 6 at 60, 5 at 80 and 2.6 at 88. Areas reset every 4–22 minutes (see World model), so even a thin supply refills, but from level 85 there's less than three levels' worth on offer per reset, shared by everyone at that level. This mirrors the mob population cliff on the Mobs page.

    16 levels have three or fewer areas to level in¶

    Up to level 15 a character can choose from 16–32 areas. 16 levels offer three or fewer, and 4 (levels 88–91) offer none at all. Area level ranges advertised in areas often don't match the mobs inside (see Mobs), so this counts areas by the mobs they actually hold.

    UnderDark carries levels 16–23¶

    Most levels draw on many areas, but a few areas carry whole stretches. UnderDark supplies 30% or more of the XP on offer at 8 levels (16–23). The 9 areas that matter most across the whole range are shown; everything else is grouped as other areas. A level stretch that leans on one area depends on that area's design.

    Some of the toughest areas are a few steps from recall¶

    Every area is measured from the Midgaard temple, where recall leads, along room exits. The farthest are Japan (42 steps, mobs around level 70), The Valkyrie (42 steps, mobs around level 63.5) and Sethanon (36 steps, mobs around level 65). Some high-level areas sit right next to recall: The Heavens (3 steps, level 72) and Dragon Cult (4 steps, level 79). Distance isn't a problem in itself (portals and spells shortcut it, and aren't followed here), but newcomers wandering out of Midgaard can walk straight into high-level content.

    59 rooms can't be reached on foot¶

    59 rooms in 20 areas have no walking route from recall, and 33 mob resets place mobs in them. Some are deliberate (immortal rooms, rooms reached by portal, mob program or a one-way trap), others may be broken exits worth checking.

    Method¶

    How these numbers are calculated
    • Suitable area for level L: an area holding at least MIN_MOBS combat mobs (population) within ±2 levels of L.
    • XP on offer: for a level-L character, the XP of killing every such mob once in a fully reset world (Combat's xp_compute model), in levels' worth (÷ 1,000 XP per level for a human).
    • Distance: walking steps from the Midgaard temple (room 3001, where recall leads) through room exits, ignoring doors' locked state; portals, mob programs and other transport aren't followed.
