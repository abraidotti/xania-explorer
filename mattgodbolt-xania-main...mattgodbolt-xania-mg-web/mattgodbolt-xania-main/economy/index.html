    Gold per kill grows 30× from level 5 to 91¶

    A kill pays about 192 gold at level 5, 1,362 at 30, 2,245 at 60 and 5,742 at 91, counting the mob's gold and the 2-per-level a corpse sacrifice pays. With the kills per level from Combat, a character earns about 2,843 gold during level 10 and 42,975 during level 80. The jagged line reflects a handful of very rich mobs (see the last section).

    The healer's flat prices hit new characters hardest¶

    The healer charges the same at every level: 1,000 gold for cure light and 5,000 for heal (healer.cpp). That's 26 kills' worth of gold for a heal at level 5, 5 at 20 and 1.0 at 90. Bought healing costs new characters dearly and heroes almost nothing.

    Shop gear tops out at level 35¶

    Buying the best armour shops sell for every slot costs 13,036 gold at level 5; a character has earned that much only by level 10. Shops add nothing better after level 35: their best kit stays at 84 AC, while the best gear in the world reaches 407 by 91 (see Equipment). After the early levels, shops stop mattering for gear.

    Gold piles up with little to spend it on¶

    By level 91 a character has earned about 1,586,305 gold. Buying one of every item every shop sells would cost 1,006,210, leaving 580,095, enough for 116 heals. The healer's prices don't rise with level and shops sell no upgrades after level 35, so late-game gold has little to buy. Pets and trading between players aren't modelled.

    14 mobs carry outsized gold¶

    14 combat mobs carry more than 5× the median gold for their level, most of them in Den of Monsters (10) and Fire Newt Mountain (2). The richest relative to its level is The Mummy (Den of Monsters, level 75) with 50,000 gold, 43× the norm. Because these mobs respawn, they act as gold farms.

    Method¶

    How these numbers are calculated

    Income per kill = the mob's gold (create_mobile rolls gold/2 … 3·gold/2, mean = gold) + sacrificing its corpse (2 gold per mob level, act_obj.cpp do_sacrifice). Gold per level = income per kill × kills per level (Combat). Selling loot to shops isn't counted (floor items are worth 0; drops sell at the shop's sell %, if it trades that type).

    Spending: shop stock (price = cost × the shop's buy %, act_obj.cpp get_cost) and the healer's flat price list (healer.cpp).

    • Kills per level and the mobs fought are those of the Combat model (human, ±2 levels).
    • Shop kit: the best-AC item per slot that shops sell at or below the level; its price is what it would cost to buy it all at once.
    • Mob gold outliers: combat mobs carrying more than 5× the median gold of mobs within ±2 levels (and more than 100).
